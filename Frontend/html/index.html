FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions? We Have Answers

Understand lawyer verification, appointments, payments, refunds and document privacy.

01 How do I book a consultation?
Choose a legal category, select a verified lawyer and click Book Now. Select date, time and consultation mode, describe your problem and continue to payment.
02 How are lawyers verified?
Lawyers provide personal, educational, professional and Bar Council information. Their account remains pending until Admin review.
03 What if my lawyer rejects the appointment?
The lawyer must provide a rejection reason. The client receives a 100% refund. NyayaSetu keeps no platform share and the lawyer receives no payout.
04 When does the lawyer receive payment?
The lawyer's 90% share is released after the consultation is completed. NyayaSetu accounts for the remaining 10% platform share.
05 Who can access uploaded legal documents?
Confidential appointment documents are intended only for the client who uploaded them and the lawyer assigned to that appointment.
